## Design
- Brand palette only: PayPal blue, white, black. High contrast.
- Black/white vector world map (no detail) = graphic and readable; one accent (dashed blue route).
- Avatar pins: circular photo, white ring, small flag badge bottom-right.
- 3D device on pure black for the "product moment".

## Motion / pacing
- Rhythm is **music-driven**: strobes and cuts land on beats (88, 90, 115).
- Camera traveling along a path (route following) gives a sense of distance = "anywhere".
- Logo assembly from parts with motion blur in ≤ 10f.
- Ending on a push-in move (no settle) → seamless loop feel.
