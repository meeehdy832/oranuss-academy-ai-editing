# v02 — "Liquid Glass" iOS concept · 1280×720 · 30fps · 481f (16.0s)

## Story
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–150 | slow cinematic open (5s!) | black. "Liquid Glass" small label + giant "7:26" in liquid-glass digits fade up from blur; a magenta/purple light arc rises behind them (like a sunrise glow under the digits); camera does a very slow push and drift. Only 1 change per ~1.5s |
| 150–165 | reveal device | camera pulls back; the digits are on an iPhone lock screen; bezel appears; clock ticks 7:26 → 7:27 |
| 165–185 | wallpaper | lock screen lights up with a warm photo of a person (dim, cinematic) |
| 185–230 | UI | glass setting cards slide up into the phone: 2 info rows (icon tile + 2-line text), a slider, a "Refraction Effects" toggle (green). Caption "Customize your Liquid Glass" fades in under the phone, words bolding one after another (reading highlight) |
| 231 | hard cut | black → **white** stage (the only cut; mood switch dark→light) |
| 231–252 | settle | phone with blue sky wallpaper, translucent pastel squares (blue, green) float and drift around it then dissolve (glass shards) |
| 255–279 | split | "Controls become" types in at the right of the phone (phone on left third) |
| 279–282 | expand | phone screen scales up to fill the left 50% (split-screen layout), bezel disappears |
| 282–318 | word swap | "Controls ___" — the last word swaps: **softer** (light, gray) → **deeper** (bold, black) → **personal.** (regular); old word dissolves into tiny particles/blur; the slider on the left moves in sync with each word |
| 318–336 | collapse | left panel slides to center, shrinks into a rounded card → becomes a glass bubble |
| 336–357 | bubble → logo | glass sphere shrinks to a dot, then morphs into the Apple logo; color shifts sky-blue → gray → black |
| 369–410 | sign-off | "this is a concept." small, center |
| 416–470 | credit | "by Oly :)" small, fades out at the end |

## Design
- Two acts with opposite palettes: Act 1 near-black (#050307) with one magenta/violet glow light source; Act 2 pure white with a ghost watermark (huge faint shape at ~3% opacity).
- Liquid-glass look: translucent fills, 1px light inner border, soft inner highlight at top, background visible but blurred through it.
- Typographic semantics: the swapped word's **weight/color expresses the meaning** (softer = light gray, deeper = bold black).
- Split-screen composition in 16:9: visual left 50%, copy right 50%, copy left-aligned at the vertical center.
- Small type for sign-offs (≈14px on 720p): understated, Apple-keynote voice.

## Motion / pacing
- Pacing is **slow-fast-slow**: 5 s of slow glow (mood), then quick UI beats every 0.5–1 s, then a calm 3.5 s sign-off.
- Glows animate continuously (the light arc slowly rises and breathes) so slow shots never freeze.
- Device reveal by **pull-back** (start on a detail, end on the whole object).
- Shape morph chain: panel → card → bubble → dot → logo, with color interpolation along the way.
- Word swap = blur+particle dissolve out (≈4f), new word fades in from blur (≈6f), synced with a UI control changing.
