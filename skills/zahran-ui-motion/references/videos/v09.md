## Design
- Palette: off-white (#EDEEEB), teal-green (#3F9479), deep charcoal widgets (#1E1F1F), mint chart (#3FD39A), sparse orange/purple data accents.
- Widgets = Apple watch/iOS squircles, big numbers (64px on 720p), small gray units.
- Folder icon + filename label above each card (Finder metaphor, same as v01).
- Grid crosshair decoration in the green statement scene: subtle technical texture.

## Motion
- **Pixel dissolve** is used once, at the act break (green world → light chat world).
- Whip-pan to reveal the off-screen end of a long UI (input → send button).
- Deck stacking for a sequence of widgets: new on top from the left, old pushed right/back.
- Two speeds: the collage section is fast and busy; the "Meet Vidaya" section is calm (3 s of minimal motion) — contrast in pacing.
