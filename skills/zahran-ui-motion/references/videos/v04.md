# v04 — "Pool" (find the source of a screenshot) · 720×1280 · 30fps · 382f (12.7s)

## Format
Wide content band (≈16:9, 720×390) centered in a 9:16 frame; above/below = blurred enlarged copy of the band (ambient fill, same trick as v03). Handle under the band.

## Design
- **Thematic background**: an app called Pool → pool-water caustics and rubber ducks. The background tells the brand story.
- Serif display (Instrument-Serif / Editorial style, bold, tight tracking −2%) in indigo #3B44D1 on lavender white → editorial, premium feel. UI pills in sans.
- Symmetric flanking words around a central device.
- Pale tints (lavender, periwinkle) for secondary copy; saturated royal blue only for the hero scene.

## Motion
- Persistent object through a wipe = continuity (eye stays on the thumbnail while the world changes).
- Hand cursor (grab hand) used for drag interactions; dashed connector line draws while dragging.
- Floating objects have **depth of field** (closer = bigger + blurrier) and slow rotation.
- Hero text enters word by word with blur (≈5f per word, 8–10f apart).
