# v14 — AI browser search concept (arc-logo brand) · 720×720 · 29.97fps · 446f (14.9s) · by dnyxstudios

Loop of ~6.7 s played twice (0–200, 200–400) + end card.

## Story (one loop)
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | icon → screen | a blurred purple app icon expands into a phone **screen** (white app UI, purple header) |
| 4–22 | island descends | the black Dynamic Island slides down from the top of the frame and docks above the screen |
| 22–34 | device wraps | the island grows into the phone's black body which wraps around the screen (device builds **around** the content); a blue glow rim flashes along the phone edge (AI glow) |
| 34–70 | phone hold | iPhone with a browser home: date, suggestion rows, "Discover" news card; slow push |
| 70–78 | push-through | camera dives into the screen (scale-through) |
| 78–108 | discover | zoomed view: suggestion list + news card with a translucent "AI news today" search pill; cursor clicks the search icon (press) |
| 108–165 | results | Google-style results page builds: search bar "AI News today", tabs (AI Mode / **Search** / Images / Videos / Shopping) with underline, result blocks (gray favicon circle, site name, URL, blue title, gray snippet). Cursor hovers a URL → URL highlights (blue selection) → tooltip "Copy Link" appears |
| 165–172 | **glitch-flash** | 1 frame of RGB static, 1 white, 1 black card with a white light flare, then the logo with 1-frame black block flashes |
| 172–200 | logo | arc logo (three concentric curved strokes) small at center, rotates slightly, holds |
| 402–445 | end card | dnyxstudios with radial blur-in |

## Design
- Pure white, real UI fidelity (Google results typography: 18–20px blue titles #4B5BD8, gray snippets, light gray search pill #F3F3F3).
- The device is presented black on white — maximum contrast silhouette.
- Purple brand header only on the phone home → the brand color appears just at the start.

## Motion
- **Device builds around content** (island → frame) = premium "product reveal" alternative to just sliding a phone in.
- Hover → highlight → tooltip micro-sequence (≈20f) shows "intelligence" without words.
- Glitch-flash transition = 6–7 frames of alternating noise/white/black/flare — maximum energy, used once per loop, right before the logo.
- Calm sections have 0 cuts; the energy is concentrated in 1–2 transitions.
