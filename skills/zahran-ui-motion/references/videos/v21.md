## Design
- Black / near-black (#050407), warm titanium phone, Siri gradient glow (violet #7B5CFF, magenta, amber).
- Text: SF Pro, white, small (≈22px on 720) — Siri voice, not shouty.
- Bokeh dots in a ring = calm brand sign-off (like a Siri/AI presence).

## Motion
- **3D device rotation** reveal + exit (a premium product beat).
- Domino stagger (1f per item) along a diagonal path = energy without chaos.
- Typing as the main storytelling (request → reply).
- Pacing: fast hook (rise in 5f), slow read (2 s), fast spin (8f), quick portfolio burst (2 s), slow sign-off (2.5 s).
