# v23 — "how is the new siri?" (macOS Spotlight/Siri concept) · 720×720 · 30fps · 502f (16.7s) · by creatoroly

Loop of ~8.2 s played twice (0–246, 246–499).

## Story (one loop)
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | dark | near-black with a faint diagonal sheen (dark glossy wave texture) |
| 8–28 | question | "how is the new siri?" types word by word, small white text center; "new" first appears blue then white |
| 28–36 | **light sweep** | a bright white light streak appears at the end of the sentence (a comet) |
| 36–48 | **sweep-eat** | the comet becomes a small circular glyph (the Siri orb ⊖) and travels **right → left**, the words compress and get swallowed into it (text collapses toward the orb, motion blur), until only the orb remains |
| 48–84 | orb | a white line-art orb icon at the center, slowly zooming in; background sheen moves |
| 84–90 | activate | the orb becomes a glossy 3D glass sphere with a soft halo ring (lights up) |
| 90–128 | cursor | arrow cursor glides in from bottom-right, turns into a hand over the orb, clicks (orb presses) |
| 128–138 | **portal** | the background turns into the macOS Sequoia-style wallpaper (silky beige/lavender waves) as the camera flies through the orb — the orb shrinks into a pill |
| 138–165 | spotlight | the pill expands horizontally into a dark glass **Spotlight bar**; placeholder "Search or Ask" (gray), a waveform icon left, a mic icon right |
| 165 | snap zoom | instant ×2 push on the bar (flow spike 12) |
| 165–185 | type | "creatoroly" types big, the camera pulls back to normal (ease-out), "— Open" suggestion appears in gray inline highlight |
| 185–215 | result | memoji avatar appears at the right end of the bar; a second smaller pill drops below: [app icon] "creatoroly.com" |
| 215–240 | hold | gentle drift |
| 240–246 | fade | to black → loop |

## Design
- Dark glass UI on a premium soft-wave wallpaper (warm beige → lavender → slate) = macOS look.
- Bar: #111 at ~85% with a 1px light top border, radius = full pill, 56px tall on 720; text white 22px; secondary gray.
- The memoji appears as the "result avatar" → personal branding signature.

## Motion
- **Comet sweep that eats the sentence** into an icon — text → symbol transition (great hook-to-product bridge).
- Orb click → portal into the wallpaper world.
- Snap-zoom on the moment of typing then ease-out pull back (keeps focus on the typed word).
- Loop-friendly: ends black, starts black.
