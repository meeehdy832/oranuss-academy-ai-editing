# v18 — Find My concept "Lost your friends again?" · 720×720 · 30fps · 248f (8.3s)

## Story
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–14 | copy | vivid iOS-blue gradient sky (#2F7BF5 → lighter top-left, with soft moving white light leak); "Lost your friends again?" builds word by word (white, semi-bold, gray→white) |
| 14–30 | cursor | arrow cursor flies in from bottom-right with motion blur, curves (arc path) and approaches the word "friends" |
| 30–34 | **swipe-erase** | the cursor swipes through "friends" — the word smears into a motion-blur streak in the cursor direction and disappears |
| 34–38 | inline swap | 3 memoji avatar circles pop into the gap where "friends" was (stagger 1–2f), the cursor exits right with blur; sentence re-spaces to fit |
| 38–52 | hold | sentence "Lost your [avatars] again?" with slow push-out |
| 52–60 | scatter | avatars fly apart to different positions, words fade; blue background **ripples/liquifies** |
| 60–70 | map reveal | the liquid blue resolves into a light Apple-Maps map of London (river, labels); avatars land as map pins with colored rings |
| 70–118 | slow push | camera slowly pans/zooms the map (ease in-out) |
| 118–126 | shrink | the full map **shrinks into a rounded widget** (squircle) at the top center, very fast (flow peak 7), with blur |
| 126–180 | copy 2 | "find everyone" (black) under the map widget; avatars orbit around in an ellipse, each with a colored ring (green/purple/red/blue) and slight depth blur; a faint dot grid on the white background |
| 180–188 | **gather** | avatars accelerate (streaks) into a cluster above, map widget fades; "find everyone" → "with find my" (words swap one at a time with vertical offsets) |
| 188–247 | end | 2×3 grid of avatars above a green Find-My location dot (pulsing ring, rotating light cone); "with find my" small; quiet hold 2s |

## Design
- iOS blue sky gradient → white with dot grid → white. Blue/green only.
- Memoji avatars in circles with a 3px colored ring (each friend a hue) = colorful accents on neutral maps.
- Apple Maps light style for authenticity.
- Lowercase, short copy: "find everyone", "with find my".

## Motion
- **Swipe-erase replacement**: the cursor's motion literally wipes a word and objects take its place — great storytelling device (text → visual).
- Map → widget shrink (scene becomes a component) and liquid ripple transitions.
- Orbit idle animation around the copy; gather/cluster at the end = "everyone together".
- Velocity graph: slow drift (0–110) → sharp peak (120) → slow → peak (185) → calm. Two big moves only, everything else gentle.
