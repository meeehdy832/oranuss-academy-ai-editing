## Story
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–16 | hook | dark navy (#050B16 → #0A1A2E bottom glow). "wait…" types letter by letter; tiny album-cover squares float upward like particles (bokeh, at different blur levels) |
| 17 | **white flash** | 1 frame white (with the scene ghosted) — beat hit |
| 18–55 | question | "what is" / "this song?" two lines, small white; album squares drift in the lower half |

| 260–266 | flash | blue logo fills; 2 frames of light gray flash; cut to white |

## Design
- Dark navy + Shazam blue (#0B6BF6) glow; white SF Pro type, small sizes (≈20px on 720) — confident, quiet copy.
- Fan/arc of devices = "catalog / abundance" composition (center phone biggest, sides lower and rotated).
- Ending flips to white for the brand lockup — the brightest frame of the video lands on the logo.

## Motion
- Lyric-per-beat word swaps on the phone screen (beat-locked kinetic typography).
- A particle drawing a logo path (stroke-dash animation) = brand reveal.
- 1-frame white flashes as punctuation at act changes.
- Phone tumbling with constantly changing tilt keeps a long section energetic without cuts.
