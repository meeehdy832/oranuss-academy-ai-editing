16:9-ish white band in the middle of a vertical frame; top/bottom bands are a blurred ambient copy (+ a colored blob light: orange, blue, green depending on the scene). Header text "launch vid for Maverick" (iMessage blue) sits on the top band for the first 6 s.

## Design
- iMessage design system exactly: blue #3C8CF5 sent bubbles with white text, gray #E9E9EB received bubbles, tail shapes, sender name labels 12px gray above, 32px avatars.
- Mascot as the brand character (black on one part, blue fluffy on another) — personality.
- Colored ambient blobs (orange / blue / green) bleeding from the edges set the mood of each section.

## Motion / pacing
- A long video (37 s) stays watchable by **always having text appear** (bubbles typing) — a new micro-event every 10–20f.
- Bubble entrance: scale 0.6 → 1 from the tail corner (transform-origin at the tail), spring with small overshoot, ~8f; width grows as the text types.
- Camera rhythm: push in on the newest content, pull back to show context, every ~3–4 s.
- Slot-roll for the list of platforms (vertical roll with blur, ambient color changes per item).
