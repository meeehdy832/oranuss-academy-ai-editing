# v24 — Apple Books concept "When did you last read?" · 720×720 · 30fps · 222f (7.4s) · by stepanvfx

## Story
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | open | bright sky with clouds (#3FB4E8), camera already moving (push + tilt up with ease-out — flow peak at frame 2) |
| 3–30 | question | "When did you last read?" — white **serif** (book-like) text types word by word above a frosted **macOS dock** with 5 icons (Safari, Clock, Books, Reminders, Figma); the clock hands actually rotate |
| 15–30 | cursor | arrow cursor travels to the Books icon (curved path), hovers; the dock icon magnifies slightly (dock magnification) |
| 30–50 | hold | slow push-in, icon bounce (like a launching app) |
| 50–54 | launch | camera accelerates left/in (ease-in, flow ramps), cut on peak |
| 54–70 | phone | the Apple Books app opens on a white iPhone (book covers: "Atomic Habits", "Deep Work", "surrounded by idiots", "Why We…") — covers **slide up and in** one after another inside the screen |
| 70–96 | hold | slow drift; a book cover is highlighted |
| 96–102 | **pop-out** | the "Atomic Habits" cover **leaves the phone** and flies toward the camera with a 3D tilt and heavy motion blur, while the phone falls away |
| 102–138 | book page | the cover settles front-on; behind it, the background becomes **blurred book page text** (serif paragraphs, depth-of-field); 4 small gray chips beside the cover list the book's chapters (eye "Make it obvious", heart "Make it attractive", bolt "Make it easy", check "Make it satisfying") — they slide in with stagger |
| 138–155 | statement | "read more" small serif, center, on white |
| 155–221 | lockup | orange Apple Books icon pops (scale overshoot) + "books x stepanvfx" small gray under it; long hold (2.2 s) |

## Design
- Serif typography (New York / Tiempos-like) chosen because the product is **books** → typeface communicates the subject.
- Sky + frosted dock = macOS world; white phone UI; final white + orange icon.
- Background texture made of the subject matter (book page text, blurred).

## Motion
- Camera already moving on frame 0 (no static open).
- Object leaving its container (cover → out of the phone) as the hero transition, with 3D tilt + blur.
- Dock magnification + bounce = authentic micro-interaction.
- Short (7.4s): 3 scenes + logo. Very efficient storytelling: question → app → content → CTA.
