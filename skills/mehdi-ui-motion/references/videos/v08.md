## Design
- Pale blue-white (#EEF3FB) → pure white stage. Mascot in pure black = maximum contrast focal point.
- Headline font: wide grotesk (Clash/General-Sans-like), tight tracking, black; accent words blue (#2D5BD7).
- Ghost background typography (very large, very faint) adds depth without noise.
- Illustrated b/w avatars to match the black mascot → coherent monochrome system, blue for interaction only.

## Motion
- **Text scramble/decoder** effect for the CTA word (~12f of random glyphs at 1 change/frame, then lock).
- Elements emerge from behind other elements (masking with gradient) instead of popping.
- Orbit/satellite motion as idle animation.
- Implosion (things sucked into the hero) as a transition into the next beat.
- Fast, dense middle (115–190: many moves overlapping), calm end (CTA + logo).
