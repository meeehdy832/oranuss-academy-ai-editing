# v07 — "There's more to this city" (travel / city-guide app) · 720×720 · 30fps · 454f (15.1s)

## Story
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–55 | hook typed | cloudy blue sky, slow drift. "may i know where you at?" — each word appears and the **camera snaps left to follow it** (5–6f whip pans per word, visible as repeated −10 px/f spikes); previous words drift out of frame |
| 55–70 | text → bubble | the sentence shrinks into an iMessage-style white bubble with avatar "Mike" (morph: loose text → UI component) |
| 70–110 | context | a small 3D airplane flies across drawing a long contrail; the bubble floats below it; camera tilts slowly |
| 110–116 | dissolve | bubble + plane fade into sky blur |
| 116–150 | window | interior airplane: blue seat (yellow headrest) on the left, an oval **airplane window** in the center; headline "There's **more** to this city" — "more" in a white rounded highlight box, black text (inverse highlight); hand cursor taps the window → window glass slides up, revealing a "Ready to explore?" hand-lettered card + black "VIP TICKET" pass with QR + a leaf in the foreground |
| 150–167 | push-through | camera pushes into the window (scale ×3 in ~15f, ease-in), headline flies out the top |
| 167–172 | portal | the window content becomes a dark city map (Vienna) with a radial darkening that opens from the center (portal/iris) |
| 172–205 | map | dark-mode map; "People made city guides" — "city guides" in bright blue; avatar stack pops; a white pill marker slides on the map |
| 205–216 | screen morph | the whole map shrinks into a **phone screen** (rounded rect) floating on light blue with snow-like particles; new copy "Discover the city's favorites" (blue accent on "favorites") |
| 216–285 | features | photo card and "Night Club" list item pop onto the phone; a dark glass card "Drake new album hotels" slides up from below; small push-in |
| 285–300 | collapse | the phone collapses into a blue circle (blur + scale down) |
| 300–336 | sign | the blue circle wobbles, becomes the creator memoji |
| 336–395 | hold | memoji small center on white |
| 395–453 | black | 2 s black tail (Instagram loop buffer) |

## Design
- One hue family: sky/cornflower blue (#4F86DB → #6FA2EE) + white type; dark navy map for contrast mid-video.
- Bold rounded grotesk headlines (white on blue), with one word in a **white pill highlight with black text** = emphasis.
- Thematic props: plane, contrail, seat, window, VIP ticket — all toy-like 3D renders, slightly soft.
- Accent "blue" word in white headline on dark map (city **guides**), (city's **favorites**).

## Motion
- Follow-cam typing: camera jumps word to word (ease-out 5f) instead of the text sliding.
- **Zoom-through portal**: push into an object (window) → its content becomes the new full-frame scene.
- **Reverse portal**: full-frame scene shrinks into a device screen (scene → phone).
- Particles (snow dots) drifting slowly on the light-blue stage keep holds alive.
- Ending with a long black tail.
