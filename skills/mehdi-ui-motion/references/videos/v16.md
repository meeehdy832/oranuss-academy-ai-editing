## Design
- Sky-photo wallpaper as hero background → calm, "digital wellbeing" mood.
- Screen-time stat typography: huge numerals (≈ 90px on 720) + small label above + small badge below.
- Bottom sheet: white, 40px top radius, grabber, centered content, iOS blue slider (#4A84DD) with a white knob line.
- Diagonal composition for the app icons — dynamic vs. a static grid.

## Motion
- **Indexed carousel**: discrete steps (move-hold-move), each move ≈ 8f ease-in-out with blur, hold ≈ 20f.
- Numbers always roll (never cut) when values change.
- Bottom-sheet entrance: fast ease-out with the camera following up (two layers moving together, different speeds = parallax).
