# v11 — Instagram concept "meet the new Instagram" · 720×720 · 30fps · 322f (10.7s)

## Story
| frames | beat | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **impact frame** | a single frame of "meet" in hot magenta with a radial zoom-echo (concentric ghost copies) — a hit-frame to grab attention |
| 1–10 | settle | "meet" sits center, magenta → violet → black over 8f; ~10 magenta/violet **pixel squares** scatter around it and fade (digital sparkle) |
| 10–30 | build | "the" then "new" fade in from blur (gray → black) — pink/peach/lilac **gradient mesh** background slowly moving |
| 30–35 | cursor block | a magenta square caret appears after "new" |
| 35–55 | wordmark | flash-cut to the Instagram wordmark (script logo in black), slow push-out |
| 55–59 | stretch exit | wordmark accelerates to the right and **stretches horizontally** (smear frame) — out in 3f |
| 59–130 | **3D photo tunnel** | photos are mapped on the inside of a rotating 3D cylinder/corridor that sweeps past the camera (continuous, very fast at start then slower — expo-out on the rotation); "go post something cool" builds word by word in gray at the center |
| 130–140 | phone flip | a phone enters edge-on (90° Y rotation) and flips to face the camera |
| 140–200 | profile | dark-mode Instagram profile on the phone; camera pushes in and tilts down the screen (slow) |
| 200–205 | push-through | phone drops out of frame, white |
| 205–240 | copy | "it'll" "work," "trust" — words dropping in from above with size/position overshoot; "trust" bold burgundy-magenta with an underline that draws |
| 240–246 | exit | sentence slides left and fades |
| 246–273 | blob | a magenta blob enters from the top-right, wobbles (squash/stretch, organic outline) toward center |
| 273–282 | logo pop | blob inflates (blur) → Instagram app icon pops in with overshoot |
| 282–321 | hold | icon on white, no motion except a subtle scale |

## Design
- Palette from the brand gradient: magenta #E1007A, violet #8A2BE2, peach, lilac. Mesh gradient background = the Instagram gradient made soft.
- Small UI type (14–18px on 720) in the middle; one magenta emphasis word at the end.
- Real photos and real device UI for authenticity.

## Motion
- Impact frame (1f) at the very start → stops the scroll.
- Smear/stretch frames for super-fast exits (scaleX 1.6 + blur for 2–3f).
- 3D tunnel = constant high-energy motion for the "content" section.
- Blob → logo morph with squash & stretch (organic, playful) — the only "cartoon" motion, saved for the brand reveal.
