## Design
- White stage, heavy black grotesk headlines (SF Pro Display Bold/Heavy), 2-line centered, tight leading (≈1.05).
- Cards: white, radius ≈ 24px, 1px #E6E6E6 border, very soft shadow; highlighted row has a deeper shadow to look "lifted".
- Pastel initials avatars (yellow, cyan, orange, pink) = playful but readable.
- Blue (#3A86F7) = interactive (buttons, wavy line). Black pill buttons for CTAs.
- Emoji used as **illustration** — cheap, universal, colorful.

## Motion
- "Active word" color: each new word arrives in the accent color and fades to the text color → reading guide.
- Black ribbon swoosh = energetic scene wipe (use once per video, at the energy peak).
- Carousel pans: the whole camera translates, outgoing scene slides off with blur, incoming slides in — one continuous move.
- Click feedback: cursor press (scale 0.9) + button squish + content swap within 3–4f.
