# Premium-Serif Reel Style

Developed by Mehdi ORANUSS | Powered by Claude

Reference: a video-editing educator who sells an editing course. This skill is distilled from 20 of the reference creator's reels (Apr–Oct 2026, plus one from Oct 2024), each studied at hook / middle / late frames.

Use it to produce **original** reels that share the reference *style*: structure, pacing, typography, graphics and CTA mechanics. Never copy the reference creator's scripts, brand names ("Ultimate Editors", "UE"), their students' names or exact on-screen lines. Swap in the user's own offer, proof and keyword.

---

## 1. The look in one paragraph

A dark, moody, premium "creator studio": the speaker sits centred in a medium close-up in front of a vertical wood-slat acoustic wall, lit by one coloured backlight (blue by default, sometimes red, green or purple). A plant sits on the left and a black podcast mic on a boom arm crosses the frame. Over that footage he layers **clean Apple-style motion graphics**: glassy cards, soft shadows, light glows, UI mockups and numbered diamond badges. On-screen text pairs **bold white sans-serif with an italic serif accent word**. Every few seconds the talking head blurs and dims, and a graphic or a "proof" screenshot slides in. Each reel ends with a **"Comment the word X"** call to action.

## 2. The five formats he rotates

| # | Format | Share | Length | Typical views | When to use |
|---|--------|-------|--------|---------------|-------------|
| A | **Talking head + motion-graphics overlays** (the main format) | ~55% | 45–70s | 20K–87K | Teaching, frameworks, case studies, offers |
| B | **Light "canvas" explainer**: animated UI on an off-white grid, little or no face | ~15% | 50–65s | 15K–36K | Tool and AI workflows, design theory, branding |
| C | **Coaching-call clip in a glowing card** (Q&A series) | ~20% | 40–55s | 16K–22K | Community value, social proof, weekly cadence content |
| D | **Lo-fi relatable meme**: candid desk B-roll + one caption + emoji | rare | 8–12s | **1.6M** (outlier) | Reach and follower growth, humanising |
| E | **Legacy neon style** (2024): uppercase word-by-word captions, chrome title cards | retired | 60–120s | ~50K | Only if the user wants the "hype" look |

**What the numbers say:** design and editing *education* (A) and *case studies* (A) pull 55K–87K. Pure promo and Q&A clips (C) sit around 15–20K. The single meme (D) did ~20× everything else. A good mix is mostly A, a weekly C, and an occasional D for reach.

## 3. Script structure (format A)

Target 140–190 spoken words for a 50–65s reel. Sentences are short and conversational, one idea per line.

```
0–3s   HOOK            Number / identity / pain / urgency (see §4). Text on screen from frame 1.
3–10s  STAKES/CONTEXT  Why it matters, or who this person is ("He's the guy…", "In 2026…").
10–45s FRAMEWORK       3 numbered items: "First… / Second… / Third…" (STEP 1/2/3, PRINCIPLE 1/2/3)
                       Each item = badge + 2–4 word title + 1–2 lines of explanation + a visual proof.
45–55s PROOF / PAYOFF  Results: money figure, screenshot of win, before→after, timeline.
55–65s CTA             "Comment the word 'KEYWORD' and I'll send you <specific free thing>."
```

Case-study variant: **Hook (result) → "Here's how" → backstory (date beats such as "And on May 17th it all Paid Off") → 3 principles → his current status card → CTA.**

---

## 4. Hook formulas (rewrite them, don't copy)

- **Money + identity:** "This [age/type of person] makes $[X]/mo with [skill]… here's how."
- **Big aggregate number:** "$[big number]. That's how much [group] made using [method] in [time]."
- **Callout with the year:** "If your [designs/edits/thumbnails] still look like *this* in 2026…" (show the bad example on screen)
- **Mistake list:** "These three [edits] keep making you look like a beginner."
- **Pain:** "If you still can't [land clients / get views]…"
- **Shock payout:** "You won't believe how much this [editor] just got paid…"
- **Urgency/offer:** "[Offer] is closing FOREVER in [N] days…"
- **Roadmap:** "Here's exactly how I would [goal] in the next 90 days."
- **Meme (format D):** a first-person relatable situation plus emoji, e.g. "[relative] just found my reels, everyone act normal 🙏😭"

The hook text is on screen from frame 1 and sits in the lower-middle caption zone. Put the money figure in huge bold type, sometimes with an avatar chip beside it.

## 5. Typography and captions

- **Font pairing (signature):** clean geometric/grotesk sans (SF Pro Display, Inter or Manrope) in white, with **one emphasis word in italic serif** (Instrument Serif Italic, Playfair Display Italic or Editorial New Italic).
  Examples of the pattern: "*This* 16-year-old makes **$3,000**", "it all *Paid Off*", "*Premium* brand", "edit *viral* videos", "Nearby *Markets*", "*Second,* you learn…"
- **Hierarchy inside one caption:** small regular words + **one bold, larger key word** + one italic serif word. Mix up to three weights or sizes on a single line.
- **Accent colour:** numbers and years get a soft blue gradient (light sky → periwinkle), e.g. "**2026**".
- **Caption chunks:** phrase-based (2–6 words), 1–2 lines, centred, placed at **55–68% of frame height** (lower-middle, below the chin, above the platform UI). Don't use word-by-word karaoke captions; that's the old style.
- **Animation:** quick fade plus a slight blur-in or upward rise (≈6–10 frames). Words can stagger in. No bouncy pop-scale.
- **Shadow:** a soft dark drop shadow or glow so white text stays readable over the face.
- Format C headline: two-line bold sans question at the top ("What are the best posts for your Personal Brand?"), a small grey subtitle ("Exclusive coaching calls in [Community]"), and the footer CTA in small text under the card.

## 6. Motion-graphics library

Build these as reusable templates (After Effects, Premiere MOGRTs or Figma → AE):

1. **Diamond number badge:** a rounded square rotated 45°, filled to match the scene's light colour (blue/red/green/purple), with a white number, a thin light inner stroke and a soft glow. Next to it a small letter-spaced label ("STEP 2", "PRINCIPLE 2") above a bold title ("Built", "Package it premium").
2. **Icon trio row:** three diamond badges with white line icons and labels beneath ("Overthink · Hesitate · Saying No").
3. **Proof cards:** clean recreations of social UIs, such as an X/Twitter profile header, a community "win" post with likes and comments, a bank "Total balance $3,000 USD" card with Withdraw/Add money buttons and an animated cursor click, or a hiring post with the salary highlighted in a green marker box.
4. **Avatar chip:** a circular photo with a thin white ring and a name underneath; it pops in when a person is named ("He's the guy… *Zahir*").
5. **Progress timeline:** a horizontal line with ticks ("9-5 → Beginner editor → 2k/mo") and an avatar marker sliding along it.
6. **Slider:** a "Beginner ↔ Professional" slider with a cursor dragging the knob.
7. **Floating-cards carousel:** 3–5 vertical reel thumbnails or UI cards floating in a row or orbit behind or around the speaker, slightly tilted, with parallax.
8. **Glass title card:** frosted rounded rectangle, 1px light border, inner glow, big bold sans text (legacy style: chrome/metallic italic).
9. **Glowing frame (format C):** the call recording sits inside a rounded card with a cyan/blue (or violet) outer glow, on a deep navy→blue (or purple) vertical gradient with subtle noise. It can open on a Zoom/Meet grid of participants for social proof.
10. **Light canvas scenes (format B):** off-white background (#F4F2EC to #F7F6F2) with a faint 40px grid, floating white cards with large-radius soft shadows, an AI-chat/prompt box with a typing animation and attached PDF chip, a coral asterisk accent, blue node-graph circles with dashed connectors, logo pills in white capsules.
11. **Picture-in-picture bubble:** the speaker's face in a small circle over a full-screen graphic, so the voice stays anchored.

**Depth trick (use it constantly):** when a graphic appears, **blur (≈15–30px) and darken (−30–50%) the talking-head plate** and scale it up 3–5%. Bring it back when the graphic leaves. That's what makes it read as "premium".

## 7. Filming setup

- **Framing:** 9:16, medium close-up, subject centred, eyes at about the upper third, headroom small; graphics go in the lower 40%.
- **Background:** vertical wood or acoustic slat panels, a plant on the left, and a gaming/office chair visible. It's dark overall with **one coloured backlight** (an RGB wash behind the subject). Change the colour per series (blue = default, red = clients, green = roadmap, purple = premium).
- **Key light:** a large soft source from the front-side (octabox with grid); the face is slightly warm against the cool background, with moderate contrast.
- **Audio:** dynamic podcast mic on a boom arm, visible in frame (Rode PodMic style), which signals "pro creator".
- **Wardrobe:** plain hoodie or tee in neutral/dark colours; no busy logos.
- **Delivery:** energetic but conversational, hand gestures counting "one, two, three", slight smile, direct eye contact.
- **Grade:** lifted blacks avoided; deep shadows, teal/blue background, natural skin, light film grain or noise OK.

---

## 8. Editing rhythm

- A cut or graphic change roughly **every 1.5–3s**. Never more than about 4s of an untouched talking head.
- Jump cuts on every breath; punch-in (110–120%) on emphasis lines.
- Every concrete noun gets a visual: a tool gets its UI, a person gets an avatar chip, money gets a balance card, a list gets badges.
- Transitions: smooth blur/zoom and card slides with eased motion (ease-in-out, overshoot ≤ 5%). No glitch or whip transitions.
- Sound: subtle whooshes and UI clicks on card entrances, soft riser into the CTA, low-volume modern ambient/lo-fi bed. The voice is always dominant.

---

## 9. CTA and caption (post copy)

- On screen at the end: "Comment down the word '**KEYWORD**'…" (format C keeps it in the footer the whole time).
- Post caption: 1–3 lines. Line 1 restates the hook or the result; line 2 is the CTA.
  Pattern: `[Hook/result sentence]` + blank line + `Comment down the word "[KEYWORD]" and I'll send you [specific asset: guide / full breakdown / roadmap / tutorial / link]!`
- Keywords are one short word tied to the asset: guide, Viral, Brand, Style, client, Access (works with auto-DM tools like ManyChat).
- Educational design reels can instead use a value caption plus 4–5 niche hashtags (#videoediting #motiondesign #aftereffects #videoeditor #videoeditingtips).

---

## 10. How to respond when using this skill

When the user asks for a reel in this style, deliver:

1. **Format choice** (A/B/C/D) and a one-line reason.
2. **3 hook options** using §4 formulas, adapted to the user's niche.
3. **Script table**: `Time | Spoken line | On-screen text (mark *italic serif* and **bold** words) | Visual / graphic (from §6) | SFX`.
4. **Edit notes**: backlight colour, which templates to build, where the blur/dim depth trick happens.
5. **Post caption + keyword CTA**.

Keep scripts under 190 words. Use exactly three framework items unless the user asks otherwise. Every claim on screen needs a proof visual that the user actually has, so ask for real numbers and screenshots instead of inventing results.
