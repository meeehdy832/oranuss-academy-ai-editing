# Example: controlled bounce

## Intent
A small decorative badge drops into place, compresses slightly, rebounds once, and settles.

## Motion phases
1. Drop toward target.
2. Small compression at impact.
3. One low-amplitude rebound.
4. Settle.

## Guidance
Use a spring or custom keyframes with decreasing amplitude. Do not use this motion on chart values, essential text, or controls where overshoot could imply a different state. Provide a reduced-motion alternative.
